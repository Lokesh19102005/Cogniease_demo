THE CHALLENGE

Some student needs are hard to see from the outside.

Asking for help can feel difficult, and one-to-one support alone may not show the broader patterns students experience across campus. CogniEase is exploring practical ways for students to reflect and reach out, while helping institutions listen to concerns at a campus level.

01

Making it easier to speak

A student may want to share a concern without starting with a formal appointment or attaching their name to a campus issue.

02

Seeing beyond one conversation

Institutions can benefit from a broader view of reported mood patterns and campus problems, alongside the support they already provide.

03

Designed to listen

TARU brings student check-ins, ways to connect with support, and institution-facing mood insights and campus problem reporting into one product.

How TARU responds

This is the problem CogniEase is exploring; it does not suggest that every student or institution has the same experience.
